Forged ironwork

01The forge

At 19 I started an apprenticeship at The Harbour Forge in Cape Town. On the side I did welding jobs around a climbing gym in exchange for a membership. After the forge I opened my own steel fabrication shop, Struk. That climbing gym was Bloc 11, and they've hired me three times since, most recently as sole designer of their 900 m² bouldering gym in Fourways.

02The degrees

I did a BCom at IMM, then went to the Netherlands for my BSc in Industrial Design Engineering at The Hague University of Applied Sciences. After all those years in workshops I can't really draw something without already thinking about how it's going to get made. But when ideating I do try to postpone judgement and let creativity flow.

03Product design

Product design is what I love. If there's a physical problem to solve, in any industry, I want to build the answer. I'm happiest at the helm of a product development project, pulling the engineering, manufacturing, user needs and cost together into one product that makes sense. A lot of my work so far has been one-offs, which is why FULLER, a drink dispenser for elderly care, matters so much to me.

04Hands-on

I still love the hands-on technical work too. I think things through, and I don't like doing anything without a reason behind it. I stay with a problem until it works and can actually be made.

05The unknown

I go after projects that push past what I can already do, and that usually means learning new tools and software. The unknown doesn't scare me. Working through it is what my degree trained me for.

06Off the wall

I've been climbing since I was 13, which probably explains all the climbing gyms. Off the wall I'm usually tinkering with my 3D printer or halfway through a DIY project.
